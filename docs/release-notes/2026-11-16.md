# Turn 16 Release Notes — Nov 9–15, 2026

Weekly digest compiled from merged pull requests and closed issues in `HandsUpBilly/BBT`.

**32** PRs merged (12 shipped to production `main`, the rest to `staging`) · **9** issues closed

A big content week: Series 2 and the new twelve-puzzle Blood Feud series are now live, alongside interactive split points on the pitch that let you jump between parallel universes straight from the dice. Crowd-surf puzzles got smoother — pushes into the crowd now resolve on their own — and puzzle-disabled actions are clearly marked with a lock. The portrait Play Diagram bug is fixed and several puzzle-authoring safety checks were added.

## New

- **Series 2 and Blood Feud are live.** Series 2 ("The Troll Toll") expands to eight puzzles mixing Reikland Humans and Imperial Nobility against Black Orcs, and the new Series 3, **Blood Feud**, adds twelve advanced Human vs Black Orc puzzles (nine touchdowns, three crowd surfs) with its own crest. Puzzles across both series were rebalanced so most boards offer at least two competitive lines. Existing scores and puzzle IDs are preserved. ([#372](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/372), [#358](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/358), [#359](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/359), [#366](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/366), [#368](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/368), [#337](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/337))
- **Split points on the pitch.** Each block that split the run into parallel universes now shows a small badge on the pitch. Tap it to see the dice faces behind each outcome and switch to that universe, including nested splits. ([#356](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/356))

## Improved

- **Crowd surfs resolve automatically.** In crowd-surf puzzles, a legal push off the pitch now happens on its own, with no separate "Push into crowd" button. A universe where no one can still Block or Blitz is conceded automatically, so you're never left stuck. ([#336](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/336))
- **Locked actions are marked.** Actions a puzzle turns off now show a lock icon in the action menu, so you can tell them apart from actions that just aren't possible on the current board. ([#339](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/339))
- **Puzzle Creator blocks unwinnable puzzles.** The editor now refuses to save a puzzle with no actions enabled, or with no action that could reach its objective (e.g. a crowd surf with Block and Blitz both off). Older puzzles like that now end in a proper "Drive failed" screen instead of freezing. ([#343](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/343), closes [#340](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/issues/340))

## Fixed

- **Play Diagram flipped on phones.** The end-of-run Play Diagram now matches the pitch orientation you actually played in, so portrait runs on phones no longer appear transposed. ([#338](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/338), closes [#293](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/issues/293))
- **Mismatched dropdown shadows.** The Legend and Action Log dropdowns now share the same lighter shadow as the other toolbar menus, and several stale theme styles were cleaned up. ([#346](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/346), [#347](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/347))
