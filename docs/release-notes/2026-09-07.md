# Turn 16 Release Notes — Aug 31–Sep 6, 2026

Weekly digest compiled from merged pull requests and closed issues in `HandsUpBilly/BBT`.

**49** PRs merged · **7** issues closed · **v1.0.2, v1.0.3, v1.0.4** shipped

A big week for identity and connection: public avatars and nationalities on the leaderboards, Discord and passwordless email sign-in alongside Google, and a full redesign of the sign-in dialog, victory screen, and branch-review tree — plus two new playable rosters and a run of fixes to hover glitches, orientation bugs, and layout gremlins.

## New

- **Public avatars and nationalities on the leaderboards.** Signed-in players can upload an avatar (or use their Google photo) and set a country, both now shown on puzzle and series rankings — fixing the long-standing report that avatars weren't appearing there. ([#273](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/273), closes [#136](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/issues/136))
- **Sign in with Discord or email.** Alongside Google and guest play, you can now sign in with Discord or a passwordless email link, all through a redesigned, focused login dialog. ([#306](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/306), [#308](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/308))
- **Weekly release notes, right in the app.** A new "Release Notes" button under the version number in the About dialog opens a click-through history of what's changed each week. ([#264](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/264))
- **Recursive chain pushes.** Pushing a player into an already-occupied square can now chain through multiple players in one go, with the full chain shown in pitch trails, action logs, and play diagrams. ([#298](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/298))
- **New failure and warning dialogs.** A branded dialog now appears when your ball carrier can no longer score, with clear restart/exit options; and after a touchdown, you're warned if any Parallel Universe branches are still unfinished. ([#290](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/290))
- **Two new rosters.** Black Orc and Imperial Nobility teams are now fully supported, with complete rosters, positional limits, and dedicated portrait art. ([#294](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/294), [#296](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/296))
- **Cleaner branch outcomes.** Parallel Universe branches that resolve to the same result are now grouped into one merged outcome, labeled with the dice that produced it, with every matching route highlighted for review. ([#265](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/265))

## Improved

- **Touchdown and branch-tree redesigns.** The touchdown victory dialog, the player-comparison matchup panel, and the branching game tree all got visual overhauls for clarity and consistency. ([#280](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/280), [#286](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/286), [#299](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/299))
- **Tidier branch and series cards.** Parallel-universe cards now size to their own content instead of stretching to fill space, series cards no longer jump on hover, and series matchup text was simplified. ([#272](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/272), [#287](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/287), [#301](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/301), [#302](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/302))
- **Clearer tutorial goal.** The opening Tutorial briefing now states the actual objective up front — find the sequence with the highest chance of meeting the stated goal. ([#270](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/270))
- **Report an issue, always reachable.** Problem reporting moved into the account menu so it's available mid-game, not just from a settings screen. ([#290](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/290), [#294](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/294), [#298](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/298))
- **Sign-in polish.** Landing sign-in buttons were aligned and evened up, and the provider list in the new login dialog was refined for a calmer, consistent look on desktop and phone. ([#283](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/283), [#308](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/308))

## Fixed

- **Slow first load.** Hero background images shipped as ~7.8MB of PNGs; they're now WebP, about 90% smaller, with no visible difference. ([#260](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/260))
- **Play diagrams pointing the wrong way.** Diagrams for completed drives now match the pitch's real orientation and scoring direction on both axes. ([#270](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/270), [#277](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/277))
- **Board jumping on hover.** Hovering an opposing player's card no longer resizes or shifts the pitch. ([#281](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/281), closes [#276](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/issues/276))
- **Sign-in button glitches.** Fixed misaligned hover behavior on the Google button and removed a duplicate frame around it. ([#282](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/282), [#309](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/309))
- **Drive-failed dialog's black banner.** The header behind the "Drive failed" artwork is now transparent instead of showing an unwanted black bar. ([#305](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/305), closes [#292](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/issues/292))
- **Footer stranded mid-page.** On short screens (Settings, Help, Leaderboard), the footer now pins to the bottom of the viewport instead of floating partway up the page. ([#312](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/312), closes [#310](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/issues/310))
- **Lockstep dice mismatches.** Parallel Universe branches with genuinely identical pickup rolls now merge correctly instead of splitting apart. ([#307](https://github.com/HandsUpBilly/BBT/pull/307))

## Breaking

None this week that changes existing behavior in a way that needs action from players.
